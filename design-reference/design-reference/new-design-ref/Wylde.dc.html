01

The problem, and the box it lives in

Groups do not fail to make plans because they lack a tool. They fail because the cost of proposing something is social, and the cost of ignoring a proposal is zero. So the feed of a planning app becomes a graveyard of dead suggestions, and opening it starts to feel like a chore.

The constraint I wrote before the brief: if people will not propose a plan when nobody can see it fail, we stop. Falsifiable by five conversations, not by a quarter of building.
