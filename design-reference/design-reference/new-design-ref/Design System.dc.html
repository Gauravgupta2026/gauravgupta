02

Colour

A near-black ground, a long grey ramp for text and borders, and two accents. Red is identity only — the monogram, live dots, rules. Lavender is interaction — links, focus, selected state.

LIGHT MODE

Light mode is a remap, not a second palette: every dark hex has one ivory counterpart (shown as the small chip under each swatch). Ground is ivory #F4F2EE, never pure white — white would clash with the red and lavender and lose the paper feel. Lavender darkens to #4B47C9 to hold contrast; red is identity and never shifts. Pure black stays unmapped because it only ever appears as shadow and mask alpha.

RULES
Serif headings always weight 300 with negative tracking. Never bold serif.
Mono is uppercase only, 10–12 px, tracking 0.14–0.24em. It labels; it never carries a sentence.
Italic serif marks voice — pull quotes, “currently” notes, section asides.
Body sans sits at 18/32; long measures cap at 720 px with text-wrap: pretty.
