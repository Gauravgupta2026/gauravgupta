## Botanical footer atlas — 7 October 2026

`botanical-atlas.webp` was generated with the built-in image-generation tool for this portfolio, then optimized to 1024px transparent WebP (168,226 bytes). Generated imagery, not photography of Gaurav's work. Original retained under the image tool's generated-images directory.

Prompt: “Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: transparent botanical sprite atlas for a portfolio footer animation. Create a square image with precisely four equal square cells in a 2 by 2 layout, each cell containing one isolated object centered with generous transparent margin: upper left a single delicate pale pink rose petal, upper right a curled rose petal with a darker pink edge, lower left a slender natural sage green leaf, lower right a small curved olive leaf. Photorealistic macro botanical textures, delicate veins, translucent thin petals, irregular organic edges, gentle diffuse backlighting, understated natural colors. Actual transparent background in all four cells, no floor, no cast shadow outside objects, no labels, no grid lines, no flowers assembled together, no cartoon outlines, no illustration, no halftone. Objects must not cross cell boundaries. Designed to be individually animated over a near-black background.”
