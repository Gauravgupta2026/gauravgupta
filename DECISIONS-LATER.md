# Decisions for later

## Navigation action order and emphasis

We still need to decide:

1. Whether **Say hello** should sit on the left side of the navigation and **Resume** on the right.
2. Which action should receive the stronger visual treatment.

Evaluate this after the opening composition settles. The decision should follow the primary hiring journey: whether a visitor is more likely to need evidence first through the resume or a direct invitation to begin a conversation.

Until then, keep the current arrangement reversible and avoid treating it as a permanent design-system rule.
